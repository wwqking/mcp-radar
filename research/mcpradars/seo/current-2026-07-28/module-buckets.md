## Buckets needing your verdict

For each one the question is **not** whether individual keywords are good.
It is: *is this a category I don't cover yet, or a semantic grab-bag?*

Read the head keywords below. Several related head terms clustering together
means a real category you're blind to — keep the whole bucket. Scattered
unrelated topics means a grab-bag — drop the whole bucket.

Do not filter these by purity automatically: a module you don't cover scores
low *because* you don't cover it. That is the signal, not the noise.
